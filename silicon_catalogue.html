Who We Are

Silicon is a reputed manufacturer of construction chemicals & building materials. Our organization has proved itself as a consistent supplier of uncompromising quality products in the construction industry.

We offer a wide range of construction chemicals all around the market. We are supported with a team of highly experienced professionals who help us in offering a qualitative range of products to our clients. Our range includes Tiles Adhesive, Epoxy, Wall Putty, Tiles Grout and Cleaner.

We provide the customization facility and provide the product on high quality bases. We are accredited as an ISO 9001-2015 Certified Company by IAF and CAB. We maintain high standards in the quality of the products.

Our products are safely packaged and free from damage while transferring. Our range of construction chemicals is exclusively used in the civil engineering industry. Our customers include many prestigious government projects, private institutions, builders, and contractors.

More Products

Wall Putty

Silicon wall putty is a white cement based putty packed with functional additives. It helps give interior and exterior tough plasters an even finish along with providing great undercoat adhesion to emulsion paints.

  • Smooth & uniform finish on rough plasters
  • Easy to work with in terms of application
  • Seals walls from moisture — protects against mold & mildew
  • Good tensile, compressive & flexural strength
  • Water Retentivity: 98.63% (spec: >95%)

Tiles Grout

Silicon Tile Grout is a polymeric cement based tile grout specially designed to create non-shrink, water proof tile joints. For grouting ceramic, mosaic, natural stone, marble, etc.

  • Suitable for interior & exterior grouting
  • Can fill up to 5mm grout width
  • Non-shrink, water and UV resistant
  • Suitable for swimming pools, bathrooms, kitchens
Available Colours

Epoxy Grout

Silicon epoxy tile grout is a superior epoxy tile grout designed for use in both interior and exterior tile installation, offering long lasting durability and resistance to stains and chemicals.

  • Ceramic, Vitreous, Glass Mosaic, Metallic, Terrazzo, Natural Stone
  • Institutes, resident facility, rest rooms, schools & kitchens
  • Ready to use · Stain free · Easy to clean
  • Makes joints water resistant and UV resistant

Cement (Grey & White)

Silicon white cement is used for decorative and architectural applications — tile grouting, terrazo flooring & product precast concrete such as panels and sculptures.

  • Base for colored concrete and paints
  • Repair work — filling cracks
  • Inherent water resistance — ideal for swimming pools & water tanks
  • Available in Grey & White variants

Tap & Metal Cleaner

Silicon Tap & Metal Cleaner effortlessly removes water stains, soap scum, and hard water deposits from all types of metals, restoring shine and cleanliness without harsh chemicals.

  • All CP Fitting & Showers · Chrome Fixtures
  • Bronze Fixtures · Basin · Mild Steel Pipes
  • Sanitary Ware
  • Powerful yet gentle — safe for the environment

Tile & Stone Cleaner

Silicon Tile & Stone Cleaner is a safe & effective cleaner for daily maintenance of tile & marble flooring with its natural pH. Available in 500ml, 1ltr & 5ltr.

  • All Unglazed Ceramic Tile · Vitrified Tile
  • Glazed Ceramic Tile · Glass Tile · Stones
  • Porcelain Tiles
  • Natural pH — keeps tile/marble spotless

Block Jointing Mortar

Silicon Block Jointing Mortar is a premix high graded polymer modified thin based masonry mortar for jointing & laying of AAC Block, Flyash Bricks, Concrete Blocks, Hollow Blocks and Clay Bricks.

  • No curing required / Self curing
  • Minimize shrinkage & seepage · High Bonding
  • Environmental friendly · Cost effective · Improved workability
  • Color: Grey Powder · Packing: 20 Kg · Pot Life: >30 Min

Silicon White Adhesive

High strength, Polymer-Modified Adhesive designed for Interior and Exterior wall and floor installations. Suitable for wet areas like Swimming pools, Bathrooms, Heavy Stones, Italian Marbles, Glass, Mosaic, Fiber-cement Sheets and Concrete Surfaces.

  • Available in Silver (White) variant
  • Available in PRO+ (White) variant
  • High polymer modification for superior bonding
